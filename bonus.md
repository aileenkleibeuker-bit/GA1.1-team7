# Bonus assignment

As a bonus assignment you can practise fork-based collaboration.

1. Individually, fork your team repository to your personal GitHub account.
2. Clone your fork locally.
3. Make a small improvement in `README.md` (clarity, typo fix, formatting).
4. Push to your fork.
5. Open a Pull Request **from your fork to the original team repository**.
6. After merge, sync your fork with upstream.
